About me

I got into coding in high school through America on Tech, a program in Miami where software engineers taught us web dev and data science every week. My first real project was a mock business site about humane treatment of animals in the food industry, which I pitched at Demo Day and placed 4th.

After that I joined my school's Computer Science Honor Society and ended up running it as Activities Director. That meant weekly tutoring, organizing two hackathons, and watching our club teams place at CyberLaunch and CSTA.

Now I'm at UCF studying CS. My favorite projects are the ones that are half code and half art, which is why everything I make ends up with a pixel mascot.

After college, I want to be a full-stack developer with a focus on UI/UX, because the creative side is my favorite part of building anything. And someday, I'd love to make games.
